# Writing the Weekly Class Report

The teacher wants a snapshot they could paste into an email to the department
or skim before office hours. Data first, judgement second, action last.

## Procedure

1. **Gather before writing.** Call `class_stats` for the overall table, and
   `chart_grades` with "class" so the teacher gets the visual alongside the
   text. Do both before writing anything.

2. **Write in this exact format:**

   **Headline:** one sentence -- the single most important thing this week.

   **The numbers:** 2-3 bullets from the stats. Exact figures, no rounding
   beyond what the tool gave you.

   **Watch list:** students whose numbers stand out, and why.
   If long-term memory holds context about a student on this list (an
   accommodation, a circumstance), weigh it here -- a low score from a student
   dealing with a concussion means something different.

   **This week's action:** ONE concrete thing the teacher should do, tied to
   a number above.

3. **Interpret, don't recite.** "The midterm mean of 78.8 hides a 36-point
   spread" is a report. "The mean was 78.8" is a spreadsheet cell.

## Rules

- Never invent a score. If you don't have it, call the tool.
- Name the outliers on both ends -- the report should notice excellence, too.
- 150 words maximum, not counting the chart.
